How PTO accrual works

With accrual, you earn a little PTO every pay period: yearly PTO hours ÷ number of pay periods. Many employers also set a cap on how much you can bank, after which you stop accruing. Some states, such as California, treat accrued vacation as earned wages, so it must be paid out when employment ends. Check your employer's policy and your state's law.
