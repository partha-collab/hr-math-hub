Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version: the numbers you type into our calculators stay in your browser. We do not collect them, and we do not ask you to create an account.

What we collect

Calculator inputs. Salary, hours, state, age and other details you enter are processed on your device in your web browser. They are never sent to our servers or stored by us.

Emails you send us. If you write to contactus@hrmathhub.com, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply. Incoming mail is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing.

Hosting logs. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its DNS is provided by Cloudflare. Like any website host, they may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page. We do not control or receive that data. See the privacy statements of GitHub and Cloudflare.

Your cookie choice. We save one item, your Accept or Reject choice, in your browser's local storage (key "tc_consent") so we do not ask again. It stays on your device.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from Google AdSense. Ads load only after you click Accept on our cookie banner. If you click Reject, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we do not load ads or advertising cookies. If you accept, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, and may use information about your visits to this and other sites. You can change your choice at any time with "Your privacy choices" in the footer, and manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of many interest-based ads at optout.aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org. Learn how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not collect personal information through the calculators. If you accept advertising cookies, the advertising partners' collection may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some state laws. You can stop this by rejecting cookies, using Global Privacy Control, or using "Your privacy choices" in the footer.

Your rights

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and other states with privacy laws may have rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising or sharing. Because we hold almost no personal information about visitors (apart from emails you send us), there is usually little to access or delete. To make a request, write to contactus@hrmathhub.com and we will respond within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we use ad cookies only with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time with "Your privacy choices".

Children

The Site is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and retention

The Site is served over HTTPS and runs without a database or user accounts. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to respond and for our records.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.
