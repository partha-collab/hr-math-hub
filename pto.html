How PTO accrual works

With accrual, you earn a little PTO every pay period: yearly PTO hours divided by the number of pay periods. With 15 days of 8 hours (120 hours) paid every two weeks, you earn about 4.6 hours per paycheck. Many employers also set a cap on how much you can bank, after which you stop accruing.

Common questions

How is PTO accrued?

Most employers give a fixed number of PTO hours each pay period: yearly PTO hours divided by the number of pay periods. Some give the full amount up front, or accrue by hours worked. Check your policy.

Does unused PTO get paid out when I leave?

It depends on state law and your employer's policy. Some states, such as California, treat earned vacation as wages that must be paid at separation. Many others leave it to the employer's policy.

What does a PTO cap do?

A cap limits how many hours you can bank. Once your balance reaches the cap, you stop accruing until you use some time. Enter your cap to see it applied.
