How the conversion works

Hourly pay equals annual salary divided by (hours per week × weeks per year). A full-time job at 40 hours for 52 weeks is 2,080 paid hours a year, so $60,000 a year is about $28.85 an hour. If you get unpaid time off, lower the weeks per year to see the effect.

Common questions

How do I convert a salary to an hourly rate?

Divide your yearly pay by the hours you work in a year. A full-time job at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours, so $60,000 a year is about $28.85 an hour.

Should I count paid time off?

If your salary covers paid holidays and vacation, the 52-week figure is fair, because you are paid for those weeks. Lower the weeks per year only for unpaid time off.

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax (gross). To see take-home pay, use the paycheck calculator.
