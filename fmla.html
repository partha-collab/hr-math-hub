    The FMLA tests in plain English

    To qualify under the federal Family and Medical Leave Act you generally need to: (1) have worked for your employer for at least 12 months, (2) have worked at least 1,250 hours in the 12 months before leave starts, and (3) work where the employer has 50 or more employees within 75 miles. Public agencies and schools are covered regardless of size.

    The 12 months do not have to be consecutive, but some older work periods may not count. This is a quick screening tool, not a legal determination.

    Common questions

    What reasons qualify for FMLA leave?

    Common reasons are the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child, your own serious health condition, caring for a spouse, child or parent with a serious health condition, and certain military family needs. This calculator checks eligibility only, not whether your reason qualifies.

    Is FMLA leave paid?

    Federal FMLA leave is unpaid, but your employer may require or let you use accrued paid leave at the same time. Some states have their own paid family leave programs.

    Will I get my job back?

    In general, an eligible employee is restored to the same or an equivalent job after FMLA leave, with limited exceptions. Check with your employer or the Department of Labor.
